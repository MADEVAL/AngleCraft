## Quick Mode: B2C product

**Input:** "We sell handmade ceramic mugs."

**Assumption:** Direct-to-consumer, English-language social content.

| # | Angle | Hook | Direction |
|---|-------|------|-----------|
| 1 | Myth vs Reality | "Everyone says handmade mugs are overpriced. Here is what [X] hours of craft actually look like." | Break down the production process to justify the price. |
| 2 | Historical Lens | "This centuries-old Japanese firing technique still cannot be replicated by machines." | Tell the story of a specific craft tradition behind the product. |
| 3 | Paradox | "The more 'perfect' a handmade mug looks, the less it is worth." | Make the case for the premium value of visible human imperfection. |
| 4 | Hidden Driver | "Cheap mugs break because of a design flaw you have never noticed." | Explain the material-science mechanism behind the price gap. |
| 5 | Contrary Thesis | "Stop buying matching mug sets. Mismatched is the real luxury." | Reframe the purchase from uniformity to curated individuality. |

## Full-piece request

**Input:** "Give me an angle and write the full 1,200-word article."

**Response:**

> Here is the angle and outline first. If it works, I will expand it.
>
> **Angle:** Price of Solution
> **Hook:** "Yes, a cheaper accountant saves [$X] a year. The bill arrives at tax season."
> **Outline:** (1) the visible fee, (2) the hidden error/rework cost, (3) the decision rule for switching, (4) the one case where cheap is correct.
> Want me to write the full draft from this outline?

Do not silently produce a long draft. The skill owns the angle layer.
