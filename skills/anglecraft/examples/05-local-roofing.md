# Example: Local Roofing Company (Local Service)

**Input:** "We do roof repair and replacement in [city]. People only call after a leak."

**Mode:** Quick
**Assumption:** Local search + Facebook, homeowners.

| # | Angle | Hook | Direction |
|---|-------|------|-----------|
| 1 | Conditions | "If your roof is under [X] years, repair. If it is over [X], replacement is cheaper per year." | Give a decision rule based on age and damage. |
| 2 | Price of Solution | "The cheapest quote is not the cheapest roof. Here is what it leaves out." | Break down decking, flashing, and disposal costs. |
| 3 | Hidden Driver | "Contractors do not ghost you because they are busy. Your job looks unprofitable to schedule." | Explain how small jobs get deprioritized and how to fix the brief. |
| 4 | Myth vs Reality | "Everyone says hail damage is obvious. Here is what an adjuster actually looks for." | Replace the visual myth with an inspection checklist. |
| 5 | News Hook | "[City] changed its permit rule for roof replacements this year. Here is what it means for your quote." | Bridge the local rule to the homeowner's timeline and cost. |

`Replace [city], [X], and the permit claim with verified local data before publishing.`

**Why this mix:** Conditions captures the repair-vs-replace decision, Price handles the quote objection, Hidden Driver explains a frustrating behavior, News Hook adds local relevance.
