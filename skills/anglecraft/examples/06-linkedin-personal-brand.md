# Example: Founder Personal Brand (LinkedIn)

**Input:** "I am a B2B founder. My LinkedIn posts get no traction."

**Mode:** Quick
**Assumption:** Audience is potential buyers, not peers.

| # | Angle | Hook | Direction |
|---|-------|------|-----------|
| 1 | Level Error | "Your posts get no reach because the hook is about you, not the reader's problem." | Rewrite the first line around the buyer's tension. |
| 2 | Contrary Thesis | "Stop posting case studies. Post the decisions you got wrong." | Argue that admitted trade-offs build more trust than wins. |
| 3 | Hidden Driver | "Your best posts underperform because your network is not your buyer." | Separate audience-building from demand generation. |
| 4 | Conditions | "If you sell to founders, write on LinkedIn. If you sell to operators, write where they already read." | Match channel to buyer, not habit. |
| 5 | Paradox | "The more you polish a post, the less it sounds like a person." | Show how editing removes the signal readers trust. |

**Why this mix:** attacks the real bottleneck (audience and hook), not posting frequency. No generic "post more" advice.
