# Example: Handmade Ceramic Mugs (B2C Product)

**Input:** "We sell handmade ceramic mugs."

**Mode:** Quick
**Assumption:** Direct-to-consumer, English-language social content.

| # | Angle | Hook | Direction |
|---|-------|------|-----------|
| 1 | Myth vs Reality | "Everyone says handmade mugs are overpriced. Here is what [X] hours of craft actually look like." | Walk through the production steps that a factory price hides. |
| 2 | Historical Lens | "This centuries-old Japanese firing technique still cannot be replicated by machines." | Tell the origin story of the technique and the maker behind it. |
| 3 | Paradox | "The more 'perfect' a handmade mug looks, the less it is worth." | Argue for the premium of visible human imperfection. |
| 4 | Hidden Driver | "Cheap mugs break because of a design flaw you have never noticed." | Explain the material mechanism behind the price gap. |
| 5 | Contrary Thesis | "Stop buying matching mug sets. Mismatched is the real luxury." | Reframe the purchase from uniformity to curated individuality. |

`Replace bracketed placeholders with verified data before publishing.`

**Why this mix:** two reach angles (Myth, Contrary), two trust angles (Historical, Hidden Driver), one shareable reframe (Paradox). The price objection is answered without inventing a cost figure.
