# Scenario: Content Audit

**Input:** "Here are my last 10 posts. None got traction. Why isn't this working?"

**Mode:** Deep (existing content + "why isn't this working")

**Expected behavior:**

1. Diagnose patterns, not individual posts: hooks, audience, angle mix, platform fit.
2. Name the dominant failure mode (for example: every post opens with a definition).
3. Recommend 3-5 replacement angles with rationale.
4. If the user pasted only titles, say what else you need (first lines, audience, goal).

**Output shape:**

| Diagnosis | Angle to try | What to change | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|

Follow with a short list of next posts in the Deep Mode table.

**Do not:**

- Rewrite all ten posts.
- Blame the algorithm without evidence.
- Invent engagement benchmarks.
