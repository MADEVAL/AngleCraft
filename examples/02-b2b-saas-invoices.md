# Example: Accounts Payable SaaS (B2B, Deep Mode)

**Input:** "I run a SaaS for automated invoice processing. Our content is all feature comparisons - boring. Need a content strategy."

**Mode:** Deep
**Assumptions:** Mid-market finance teams, LinkedIn + blog, goal is qualified demos.

| Topic | Angle | Hook | Tier | Platform |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Why invoices are still manual in [year] | Level Error | "It is not a software problem. It is a trust problem." | Trust | Blog, LinkedIn |
| "AI will take accounting jobs" | Myth vs Reality | "AI will not replace accountants. It will replace accountants who only type numbers." | Reach | Twitter, LinkedIn |
| The hidden cost of cheap AP tools | Price of Solution | "Your $[X]/month invoice tool is costing you $[Y]/month in errors. Here is the math." | Conversion | Blog, Newsletter |
| When to automate vs when to keep manual | Conditions | "If invoices are under $[X], automate. If enterprise contracts, one wrong digit costs the deal." | Conversion | Landing page |
| The real reason finance teams resist change | Hidden Driver | "They are not lazy. Their bonus depends on zero errors, and they do not trust your tool yet." | Trust | LinkedIn, Video |
| How AP automation looked before cloud | Historical Lens | "Before cloud AP, one missing invoice could stall month-end close for [X] days." | Trust | Blog |
| Where AP automation goes next | Future Lens | "The next AP upgrade is not faster processing. It is [change]." | Reach | LinkedIn, Newsletter |

`Data to verify: [year], [$X], [$Y], [X], [change].`

**Why this mix:** Reach builds the category conversation, Trust handles the fear of change, Conversion answers the two buying questions (price of error, when to switch). No feature-comparison content.
